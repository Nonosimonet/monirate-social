Ten days. Twenty years. $38,879 gone 😳

$10,000 put into the S&P 500 on Jan 3, 2005 and left completely alone was worth $71,750 by Dec 31, 2024 — a 10.4% annualized return.

Same money, same 20 years, but you stepped out and missed only the 10 best days: $32,871. A 6.1% return. Ten days out of about 5,000 trading days cost you 54% of your final balance 📉

Here's the trap: 7 of those 10 best days happened within 15 days of the 10 worst days. The rebound sits right next to the panic. If you sell to avoid the crash, you almost always sell the recovery too.

Source: J.P. Morgan Asset Management, Guide to Retirement — returns based on the S&P 500 Total Return Index.
