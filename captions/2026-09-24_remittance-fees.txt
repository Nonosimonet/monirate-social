Sending $200 home costs $11. Every single time 💸

The World Bank tracks this globally: the average cost of sending $200 across borders sits around 5.7% of the amount. That's $11.36 gone per transfer — about $136 a year if you send monthly.

And here's the part most people miss: a large share of that cost isn't the fee on the receipt. It's the exchange rate margin — the gap between the rate you get and the real mid-market rate. The advertised fee can be $0 and you still pay 🤔

The UN's target for 2030 is 3%. We're not there. In some corridors it's still above 8%.

The fix is boring and it works: know the real rate before you send. If the provider's rate is 4% off the mid-market rate, that IS the fee, whatever the receipt says.

Source: World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide, global average, Q3 2025.
