In 2022 Americans got Europe at a discount. It's gone 🇪🇺

September 2022: the euro fell below parity, around $0.96. One dollar bought about €1.04 — the strongest the dollar had been against the euro in twenty years.

October 2026: EUR/USD is back around 1.12. One dollar buys about €0.89.

Same hotel, same €1,000 bill: $960 then, about $1,120 now. A 17% increase on a trip where nothing about the trip changed 🧳

This is the part of travel budgeting nobody plans for. You can shop flights for weeks and lose more than you saved on an exchange rate you never looked at — and the gap shows up again on every card payment abroad.
