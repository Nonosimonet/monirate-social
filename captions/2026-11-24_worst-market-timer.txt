He bought at four market tops and still retired a millionaire 😤

Bob is a thought experiment from Ben Carlson. He starts saving in 1970 and has the worst luck in financial history: he only ever invests right before a crash.

He buys in December 1972 — the market falls 48%.
August 1987 — it falls 34%.
December 1999 — it falls 55%.
October 2007 — it falls 57% 💀

Four purchases, four catastrophes. By retirement in 2013 he had put in $184,000 and had about $1.1 million.

How? He never sold. Not once, through four of the worst drawdowns of the modern era. His money had decades to recover and compound because he didn't interrupt it.

The fair footnote: someone who invested steadily instead of hoarding cash between peaks would have ended with roughly $2.3 million. Bad timing cost Bob half his potential — but it still didn't stop him, and that's the point. Selling would have.

Source: Ben Carlson, "A Wealth of Common Sense" — the world's worst market timer.
