Holding pesos for one year: -6% against the dollar, -25% at the shops 🇦🇷

October 2025: $10,000 bought about 14.2 million pesos at 1,420 per dollar.

October 2026: the rate is around 1,517. Those pesos are worth about $9,360.

That 6% is the small loss. The big one is domestic — Argentine inflation was running around 33% a year in 2026, so the same pile of pesos buys roughly a quarter less at home than it did twelve months ago 🛒

This is what currency risk actually looks like for the people living inside it: not a trade that went wrong, but money that quietly stops being money. It's also why dollar accounts, stablecoins and foreign assets are mainstream in Argentina rather than exotic.

If you earn, save or get paid in a volatile currency, this is your biggest position whether you chose it or not.

Sources: USD/ARS October 2025 vs October 2026; BCRA inflation data.
