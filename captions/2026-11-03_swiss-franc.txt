The boring currency that doubled your money 🇨🇭

In 2001 a dollar bought about 1.70 Swiss francs. Today it buys about 0.83.

Put $10,000 into francs back then and left it alone: roughly 17,000 CHF, worth about $20,500 now. No trades, no timing, no stock picking — just holding a different currency while the dollar lost ground against it 💵

The franc did this the way Switzerland does everything: slowly. Low inflation, big current account surplus, and a reputation as the place money hides when the world gets nervous. It touched an eleven-year high against the dollar in January 2026 before easing back.

The point isn't "buy francs." It's that the currency your savings sit in is an active position, even when you never think about it. Turkish lira holders learned the same lesson from the other direction.

Sources: USD/CHF, 2001 annual average (~1.69) vs October 2026 (~0.83).
