Fifty extra dollars a month saves about $6,600 and nearly eight years ⏳

Here's why this beats almost any investing post you'll see today: paying off a 22% debt is a risk-free, tax-free 22% return. No fund manager on earth guarantees that. The S&P 500's long-run average is roughly 10% — and it's an average, not a promise.

So the order is boring and it works: emergency cash, kill the high-interest debt, then invest.
