Japan didn't get cheaper. Your dollar got stronger 🇯🇵

2020: the dollar averaged about 107 yen. A $2,000 holiday budget bought you roughly 213,000 yen.

August 2026: the dollar sits around 160 yen. The same $2,000 now buys about 320,000 yen — 50% more money for the exact same trip 💴

Nothing about Tokyo changed. The exchange rate did all of it.

Now flip it around. If you earn and save in yen, your savings lost roughly a third of their global purchasing power over those six years — without a single bad decision on your part. Same coin, two very different lives.

Source: Trading Economics / Fed H.10, USD/JPY spot, Aug 30, 2026 (~160) vs 2020 average (106.8).
