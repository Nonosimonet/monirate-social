The safest asset on earth just fell 21% 🥶

Gold peaked at an all-time high around $5,608/oz in January 2026. Today it trades near $4,430. That's a 21% drawdown on the thing everyone buys to sleep at night.

And yet: gold averaged about $1,341/oz in August 2016. Ten years later it's up roughly 230% — $10,000 would be about $33,000 🪙

Both things are true at once. That's the whole lesson: safe never meant smooth. Every asset has a drawdown, gold included — the difference is whether you knew it before you bought, or found out after.

Sources: Trading Economics gold spot (Aug 31, 2026), LBMA/monthly averages for 2016.
