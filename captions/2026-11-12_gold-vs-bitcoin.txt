"Digital gold" and gold just spent a year doing opposite things 🪙

Last twelve months:
▪️ Bitcoin: about $120,700 → $82,400. Down 32%.
▪️ Gold: about $3,990 → $4,195 an ounce. Up around 5%.

Both are sold with the same sentence — scarce, finite, a hedge against the system. One of them behaved like a hedge this year and one behaved like a tech stock 📉📈

That's not a verdict on either. Over ten years bitcoin's return dwarfs gold's, and gold had its own 20%+ drawdown from January's record. It's a reminder that a nickname isn't a correlation: if you own both because they're "the same trade," you may be less hedged than you think, in whichever direction the next year goes.

The only way to know what two assets actually do together is to look at them on the same chart, over the window that matters to you.

Sources: spot gold and BTC, October 2025 vs October 9-10, 2026.
