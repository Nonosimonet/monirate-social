The crash that took 34 years to undo 🇯🇵

December 29, 1989: the Nikkei 225 closes at 38,915.87. The top of the biggest asset bubble in modern history.

October 2008: 7,162.9. An 82% decline, nineteen years in the making.

February 2024: the index finally closes above its 1989 record. Thirty-four years to break even 😮

And then the part nobody expected — August 31, 2026: 66,312. Seventy percent above the level that took three decades to reclaim.

Two lessons, both uncomfortable. One: "it always comes back" can mean thirty-four years, and most people don't have thirty-four years. Two: the moment everyone had given up on Japanese stocks turned out to be the entry point 📈

Sources: Nikkei Inc. historical index data; index close Aug 31, 2026.
