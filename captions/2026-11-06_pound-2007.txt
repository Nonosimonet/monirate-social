The pound bought $2.11 in 2007. Today: $1.32 🇬🇧

November 2007 was sterling's peak against the dollar. £10,000 was worth about $21,100.

October 2026: GBP/USD around 1.32. The same £10,000 is about $13,200. A 37% fall in dollar terms, spread over nineteen years.

Nothing dramatic happened on any single day — except June 2016, when the pound fell 11% in four days after the referendum and settled near 1.33, roughly where it still sits a decade later 📉

Why it matters even if you're not British: if you hold savings, get paid, or own property in one currency and spend in another, this is your largest uncovered exposure. A nineteen-year, 37% drift doesn't feel like anything month to month. It completely changes what your money is worth abroad.

Sources: GBP/USD November 2007 peak (~2.11) vs October 9, 2026 (1.3240).
