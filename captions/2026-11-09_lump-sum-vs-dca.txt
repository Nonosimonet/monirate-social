Invest it all at once, or spread it over a year? 🤷

Vanguard tested this across decades of rolling periods in the US, UK and Australian markets. Investing the lump sum immediately beat spreading it out about 68% of the time, by roughly 1 to 2 percentage points on average.

The reason is unglamorous: markets go up more often than they go down, so money sitting in cash waiting for a better entry is usually money not earning ⏳

But read the other number. Dollar-cost averaging won about a third of the time — specifically when the market fell right after the lump sum would have gone in. That's not rare, and it's exactly the scenario that makes people sell at the bottom and never come back.

So the honest answer: lump sum is mathematically better on average, spreading it out is psychologically better for a lot of people, and the strategy you'll actually stick to beats the one that's 1.5% better on paper.

Source: Vanguard, "Cost averaging: invest now or temporarily hold your cash?"
