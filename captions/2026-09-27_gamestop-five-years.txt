Five years after the squeeze: -79% 🎮

January 28, 2021 — GameStop closes at its record, $86.88 a share adjusted for the 2022 split. Reddit beat Wall Street, the movie got made, everyone had a friend who was up 400%.

August 28, 2026 — $17.87. Down 79% from that close.

The squeeze was real. The gains were real. But they belonged to a window of a few days, and almost nobody sells inside that window — because the whole point of the trade was to never sell 📉

This isn't an anti-GameStop post. It's the same pattern every cycle: the story lasts months, the move lasts days, and the people posting screenshots are never posting the exit.

Sources: GameStop close Aug 28, 2026 ($17.87); Jan 28, 2021 record close, adjusted for the July 2022 4-for-1 split.
