You own 500 companies. Seven of them are a third of your money 🎯

The Magnificent 7 — Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Tesla — now make up about 34.9% of the S&P 500's market cap (October 7, 2026). In 2018 the same seven were around 13%.

That's the highest concentration in a single cohort since the Nifty Fifty era. The top ten stocks together are above 40% of the index.

None of that makes the index bad. It makes it a different product than the one people describe. "Diversified across 500 companies" and "a third of my money rides on seven AI-adjacent megacaps" are both accurate statements about the same fund 🤔

Worth knowing before the next drawdown, not during it.
