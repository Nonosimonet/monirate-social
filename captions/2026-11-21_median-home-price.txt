Your house went up 350%. Your wealth went up 75% 🏠

US median home price in 1990: about $92,000. Today: about $410,700. That's 4.5x, and it's the number every dinner-table property conversation is built on.

Now adjust for inflation. $92,000 in 1990 is roughly $235,000 in 2026 dollars. Against that, today's $410,700 is a real gain of about 75% over 36 years — roughly 1.6% a year 📉

Still a gain. Still far less than the story. And it's before property taxes, insurance, maintenance, mortgage interest and transaction costs, which is why the lived experience of "my house made me money" is so often thinner than the headline.

This is the single most useful habit in personal finance: before you celebrate a number from the past, divide it by what the dollar did in between. Houses, salaries, your first job's rent — all of it.

Sources: NAR 1990 median existing-home price; US Census Q2 2026 median sale price; BLS CPI.
